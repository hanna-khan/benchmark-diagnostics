Featured Dermatopathology Case

Collision Lesion of Dermal Nevus and Pilar Leiomyoma

Arif Usmani, M.D.

An uncommon collision lesion demonstrating a dermal nevus and pilar leiomyoma, with histologic and immunohistochemical findings supporting both components.

Diagnosis

Left central lateral neck: collision lesion of dermal nevus and pilar leiomyoma.

The lesion extends to the deep tissue edge in the sections examined.

Microscopic Findings

The sections exhibit a circumscribed melanocytic proliferation within the dermis without significant atypia. Fascicles of smooth muscle are intermingled with the melanocytic proliferation. Sox-10 and desmin stains highlight the two components. Multiple deeper levels were examined.
