FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

01How do I submit a case?

Complete the appropriate submission form and include it with the specimen being sent to Benchmark Diagnostics. Contact the laboratory if you need assistance with submission requirements.

02What information is needed with a submission?

Required information may vary by case type. Refer to the applicable submission form and specimen guidelines, or contact the laboratory for assistance.

03How are results delivered?

Contact Benchmark Diagnostics for information regarding report delivery and access to case results.

04Do you accept STAT cases?

Yes, Benchmark Diagnostics accepts STAT cases. Please notify the laboratory at the time of submission if a case is designated as STAT.

05Can I consult with a pathologist directly?

Yes. Physicians may contact Benchmark Diagnostics for assistance with cases and pathology consultation.
