Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Benchmark Diagnostics handles information collected through this website.

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Benchmark Diagnostics respects your privacy and is committed to handling website information responsibly. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected through this website and how that information may be used.

2. Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information through website forms or other communication methods, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Subject of inquiry
  • Message or other information you choose to provide

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to inquiries, provide requested information, or communicate regarding Benchmark Diagnostics services. Please do not include patient information or confidential medical information in this form.

3. Protected Health Information

General website forms are not intended for the submission of protected health information, patient medical records, pathology reports, patient identifiers, or other confidential clinical information.

Please do not submit patient or confidential medical information through general website contact forms unless Benchmark Diagnostics specifically provides a secure method designated for that purpose.

4. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by the website, hosting provider, or analytics services.

This information may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of access
  • Referring website or source

This information may be used to understand website usage, maintain website performance, and improve the user experience.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand visitor usage, and improve website performance.

You may be able to control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain website functionality.

6. Analytics

Benchmark Diagnostics may use website analytics tools to better understand how visitors interact with the website.

Analytics information is generally used in aggregate to evaluate website traffic, page performance, and visitor engagement.

7. How Information May Be Used

Information collected through the website may be used for purposes including:

  • Responding to inquiries
  • Providing requested information
  • Supporting physician and laboratory communications
  • Improving website content and functionality
  • Maintaining website security
  • Monitoring website performance
  • Meeting legal or regulatory obligations

8. Sharing of Information

Benchmark Diagnostics does not sell personal information collected through this website.

Information may be shared with service providers that support website hosting, form processing, analytics, security, or other website-related functions when necessary to provide those services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.

9. Third-Party Services and Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites or use third-party services.

Benchmark Diagnostics is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites. Visitors should review the privacy policies of those services separately.

10. Data Security

Benchmark Diagnostics takes reasonable measures to help protect information collected through the website from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

Information submitted through the website may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, maintain business records, support operations, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

12. Children's Privacy

This website is intended primarily for healthcare professionals, business contacts, and individuals seeking information about Benchmark Diagnostics.

Benchmark Diagnostics does not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

13. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide information through the website. You may also contact Benchmark Diagnostics directly regarding questions about information you have submitted through the website.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Benchmark Diagnostics may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in website practices, services, technology, or applicable requirements.

Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
